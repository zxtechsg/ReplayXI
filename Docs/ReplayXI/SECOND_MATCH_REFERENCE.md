## Follow-up: counterattack geometry audit

Compared the current `FootballReferenceScenarios.swift` counter against the live FIFA broadcast at **36:45–36:58**, using the halfway line, centre circle, penalty area and six-yard box. These are **manual visual estimates**, not calibrated tracking. No StatsBomb coordinates were consulted or copied for this audit. Coordinates use the app's 105 × 68 m pitch: centre `(0,0)`, attack towards `z +52.5`, attacking right is positive `x`.

Three large mismatches in the version inspected:

1. **Opening field span is compressed and too central.** Molina starts by the right touchline inside Argentina's half, not `(14,-2)`. Mac Allister's first pass and Messi's touch also occur in Argentina's half. Álvarez releases from the right near halfway, not ten metres beyond it in a central lane.
2. **The final pass and shot are too deep.** Mac Allister squares from just outside the front of the area on the right. Di María meets it near the outside-left corner of the six-yard area, substantially closer to goal than the old `(-14,38)` shot. The finish crosses the keeper towards the opposite half of goal; the old negative-x target needs rechecking.

Di María is initially still in Argentina's half, then sprints from the far-side lane into the box; the old `(-23,+9)` start skips much of this run.
