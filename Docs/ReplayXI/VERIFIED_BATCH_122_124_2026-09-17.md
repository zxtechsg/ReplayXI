## Limits

Far rigs remain simplified and rigid-limbed; this is not FIFA-level art.
The package-size reduction is not a measured memory, load-time or FPS gain.

hand tracking, room placement, SwiftUI attachment layout or physical-device
performance. The Mac remained locked during this batch.
